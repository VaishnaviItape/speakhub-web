Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Speak Hub Academy

Speak Hub Academy ("we", "our", or "us") provides spoken English language instruction, classroom management, and student assessments via the Speak Hub Academy mobile application and web portals. We are firmly committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our students, teachers, and guardians.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information needed to manage student enrollment and learning progress:

  • Account Details: Student Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, and Parent/Guardian Contact details.
  • Academic Data: Batch assignments, class attendance, homework submissions, mock test recordings, and grades.
  • Billing Records: Tuition fee payment receipts, installment schedules, and transaction identifiers.

2. Mobile App Permissions & Purpose

Our Android application requests the following device permissions for educational features:

🎤 Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used strictly for student speaking exams, pronunciation practice, and oral viva submissions. Recorded audio is reviewed by mentors for pronunciation feedback.

🎧 Audio Playback (Foreground Service)

Allows students to stream and listen to English lecture recordings, sample dialogues, and listening comprehension lessons without interruption.

🔔 Push Notifications

Delivers class timetable alerts, exam announcements, homework deadlines, and attendance confirmations.

3. Data Storage & Security

All user data and audio recordings are stored on secure Google Firebase Cloud infrastructure with end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption. We never sell, trade, or monetize student personal data, contact information, or audio recordings with third-party advertisers.

4. Account & Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy, users have full control to permanently delete their account and associated personal data:

In-App Deletion:

Open the Speak Hub Mobile App > go to Profile > tap "Delete Account & Personal Data" at the bottom of the screen. This immediately wipes your credentials and marks your profile data for complete purging.

Request Deletion via Support: You may also email speakhubacademy@gmail.com with your registered mobile number. Our team will verify and permanently delete your student records within 30 days.
